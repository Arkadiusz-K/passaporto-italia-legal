1. Scope

This Policy describes personal-data processing in the Passaporto d’Italia mobile application and its information website. The application is intended for users aged 16 or over.

2. Use without an account

The application can be used locally without registration. Earned badges and local-mode settings are then stored on the device and are not sent to a cloud account. Clearing application data or uninstalling the application removes local data, subject to Android backup behaviour.

3. Data processed for a cloud account

Creating an account is optional. For a cloud account we process:

  • email address and technical user identifier;
  • optional profile name;
  • earned badges, progress, timestamps, voluntary notes and places;
  • technical data required for security, diagnostics and delivery of the service, including IP address, request information and authentication logs;
  • for Premium purchases: product identifier, purchase date, entitlement status, purchase-consent record and a SHA-256 hash of the Google Play purchase token. We do not receive card or payment-account details.

The application does not collect precise or approximate device location. Its regional map is not used to track users.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • account creation, sign-in, synchronisation and restoration — performance of a contract or steps requested before entering into one (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR);
  • security, abuse prevention and error diagnosis — legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR);
  • verification, activation and restoration of Premium and recording purchase consent — performance of a contract and proof of compliance with consumer-law duties (Articles 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(c) GDPR);
  • legal obligations, settlements, complaints and lawful requests — Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

5. Service providers

  • Supabase — authentication, database, progress synchronisation and technical logs;
  • Brevo — transactional account and security emails;
  • GitHub Pages — public hosting of legal and help pages;
  • Sentry — optional crash reporting and stability diagnostics;
  • Google Play — payment processing, purchase verification, refunds and revocations.

Providers may process data outside the EEA using applicable safeguards, including adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

6. Email

Brevo receives the recipient address and technical message data needed for delivery. We send only account, security and transaction-related messages and currently do not send newsletters.

7. Crash reporting, advertising and analytics

If crash reporting is configured, Sentry receives data needed to diagnose errors, such as stack traces, application and Android versions, device model and occurrence time. The application is configured not to attach screenshots, view hierarchy, email addresses or request contents. Data is filtered for email addresses, Supabase addresses and tokens before sending.

The application contains no advertising, advertising profiling or third-party marketing analytics.

8. Retention

Account, collection and Premium-entitlement data are retained until account deletion. Purchase-consent and limited transaction information may be retained longer where required to demonstrate compliance, handle a complaint or establish, pursue or defend claims. Technical data and backups are retained for limited periods needed for security and service continuity under provider retention settings.

9. Account deletion

The account can be deleted in the profile using “Delete account and data”. This permanently removes the account, profile, cloud collection, progress and current entitlement record. It does not automatically request a Google Play refund or erase transaction records that Google must retain. Instructions are available on the Account and data deletion page.

10. User rights

Subject to the GDPR, a user may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Requests can be sent to contact@passaporto-italia.pl. A user may also complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, or the competent authority in their EU country.

11. Security and provision of data

Connections to external services are encrypted in transit. Cloud access is restricted through authentication and access-control rules. Providing an email address is optional but required for a cloud account and Premium. Local mode remains available without it.

12. Changes

This Policy may change as the application or law develops. The current version and effective date will remain available at this address.

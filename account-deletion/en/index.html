Account and data deletion

A Passaporto d’Italia account can be deleted directly in the application or by email.

PolskiEnglish

In the application

  1. Sign in.
  2. Open the profile.
  3. Under Account settings select “Delete account and data”.
  4. Confirm permanent deletion.

Request by email

If you cannot access the application, email contact@passaporto-italia.pl from the address assigned to the account. We may request additional confirmation of account ownership.

Data removed

  • account and authentication data;
  • profile name;
  • earned badges, progress, timestamps and notes;
  • Premium entitlement and purchase-consent record, except data that must be retained by law or for handling claims.

Email requests are handled without undue delay, generally within one month. Limited information may be retained longer only where required by law, service security or the establishment, pursuit or defence of claims.

Local data

Local mode creates no server account. Its data can be removed by clearing application data in Android settings or uninstalling the application.

Deleting the application account does not cancel the Google Play purchase and does not automatically issue a refund. Consider any refund request before deleting the account.
